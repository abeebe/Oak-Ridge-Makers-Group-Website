The makerspace drive

From library meetings to our own shop

A makerspace is a shared community workshop. Members get to use tools that are too big, too pricey or too specialized to keep at home, learn from each other, and take classes. Right now we meet in a borrowed room and bring our own gear. Here's the path to a real space, and where we are on it.

  1. We are here

    Build the community

    Monthly meetings, a monthly social and show-and-tell. Every new member makes the case for a space stronger.

  2. Getting started

    Raise the money

    Grants and local supporters for rent, buildout and a first set of tools.

  3. Next

    Find a space

    A building in Anderson or Roane county with room for a shop, a classroom and storage.

  4. The goal

    Open the doors

    Open shop hours, safety classes and workshops for adults, students and families.

What we want to put in it

  • Electronics benchSoldering stations, test gear, parts bins

  • Project storageShelves so work in progress can stay put

About us

Educate & Create

The Oak Ridge Makers Group brings together hobbyists, professionals, students and tinkerers who like to build things: woodworking, 3D printing, electronics, programming, crafts and everything in between. We believe people learn best with their hands, and that the right tools shouldn't depend on having a garage full of them.

We're working toward a makerspace for Anderson and Roane counties, and we'd love your help getting there.
